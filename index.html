Our Heritage & Promise

A Taste of South India,
Right Here in Kharadi.

Gatti Chutney is built around authentic South Indian flavours, prepared fresh and served in a warm, welcoming space. Every dish draws on traditional preparation, crisp dosas made with pure ghee, and thick stone-ground gatti chutney cooked fresh daily.

  • Authentic Pure Ghee Recipes

    Recipes rooted in traditional South Indian tiffin centers.

  • Freshly Prepared & Made to Order

    Dishes griddled fresh to order, never pre-made or reheated.

  • Warm Hospitality & Clean Ambiance

    A serene, comfortable dining space with attentive service.
